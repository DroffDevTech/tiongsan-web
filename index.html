ABOUT TIONG SAN

A Familiar Name
in Shopping

For over six decades, Tiong San has been the name synonymous to the citizens of Northern Luzon with shopping, service, savings, and value.

Since its inception three generations of shopkeepers prior, up to the present, Tiong San has been the north's go-to establishment for all its wants and needs. From full-range supermarkets, to department stores offering a wide spectrum of premium to mass-market brands in practically every category, Tiong San has more than earned its moniker of "Suki ng Bayan."

OUR COMMUNITY COMMITMENT

Civic & Social Response Activities

Tiong San utilizes its proud status as the region's homegrown retail flagship to stand at the frontlines of local industry and reinforce relationships among local establishments that have become part of what is considered the "Baguio City Experience."

Through its support and partnerships with various local government agencies such as DTI-Benguet (Department of Trade & Industry), the DOLE (Department of Labor & Employment), the DENR (Department of Environment & Natural Resources), and especially the respective city and town halls of Baguio and La Trinidad, Tiong San helps promote entrepreneurship, local industry, environmental sustainability, and overall community awareness.
